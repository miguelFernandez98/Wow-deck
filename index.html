Can a PowerPoint (or Keynote) presentation be called a software product?

In most cases, a PowerPoint presentation isn't seen as typical software. Instead, it's a digital file for sharing information.

However, in Wow Deck, we create unique and customised PowerPoint/Keynote files with advanced features.

This makes them more like specialized software due to enhanced functionality, non-linear navigation, multimedia elements, and dynamic design, going beyond standard presentations, making it closer to what one might traditionally consider a software product.

How does Wow Deck differ from traditional presentations?

Wow Deck's presentations are non-linear and designed for engaging, back-and-forth conversations, which departs from traditional presentations' linear structure.

The traditional training status quo often overlooks several crucial aspects

First, training should align with business needs and start with business metrics that highlight specific problems1; however, most stakeholders requesting training cannot articulate clear and quantified training objectives, such as “x to y by when”, that need to be improved2 - 3.

Second, training should address the root causes of employee performance issues, such as skills, motivation, knowledge, procedures, tools, organizational culture, and environment1. Most cases fail to prioritize and perform an effective root cause analysis where all stakeholders are fully involved before developing any sort of solution3.

Third, traditional training programs often fail to emphasize lead metrics and accountability, mistakenly believing that "training" alone can solve everything1. Training is only part of a broader, holistic solution3.

Moreover, many training methods lack engagement and relevance4-5. They often miss hands-on activities directly related to job tasks and rely on outdated, uninspiring materials that lead to disengagement and poor retention 3.

Finally, inadequate follow-up and coaching further exacerbate these issues 6-7.
